Bias–Variance Challenge

A short but deeper quiz on underfitting, overfitting, high bias, and high variance. Focus on reasoning from training and test behavior—not memorizing definitions.

60-Second Mental Model

Underfitting

Model is too simple to learn the important signal.

Training error: high
Test error: high
Overfitting

Model learns training-specific details/noise that do not generalize.

Training error: low
Test error: much higher
High Bias

Systematic error from assumptions that are too restrictive.

Think: consistently misses the pattern.
High Variance

Model changes substantially when the training sample changes.

Think: too sensitive to the data.
Core connection: Underfitting ↔ High Bias    |    Overfitting ↔ High Variance
