What do the controls mean?

Small σ → data are concentrated near the mean → narrower and taller bell curve.

Large σ → data are more spread out → wider and flatter bell curve.

μ (mean) controls where the center of the bell curve is.

x (observation) does not control the shape of the bell curve. It represents one observation located somewhere on the distribution.
