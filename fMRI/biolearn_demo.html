Explore: Regions to Networks

Click a colored region. A region of interest (ROI) is a defined brain area from which measurements can be summarized.

Functional Connectivity

Researchers can compare how activity-related time series from different ROIs vary together.

Correlation between ROI signals does not by itself prove direct anatomical connection or causation.

Learn: MRI → fMRI → Connectivity

MRI: detailed images based on magnetic resonance properties of tissue.
fMRI: commonly uses the BOLD signal, an indirect signal related to blood oxygenation changes associated with neural activity.
Functional connectivity: statistical dependence between signals from different brain regions; Pearson correlation is a common introductory measure.

Researchers can study distributed brain systems and individual differences rather than only asking whether one region is more active.
