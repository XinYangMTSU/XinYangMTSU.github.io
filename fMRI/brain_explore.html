🧠 Lesson 1: What is your brain?

Your brain is the control center of your body. It helps you think, learn, remember, move, see, hear, speak, and feel emotions. Even while you sleep, your brain is busy!

Major parts of the brain — Johns Hopkins Medicine
👀 Look at the picture:

The cerebrum is the big upper part. The smaller wrinkly part at the lower back is the cerebellum. The brainstem is the stalk underneath that connects toward the spinal cord.

Now compare these shapes with the real MRI later in the lesson.

Cerebrum

seh-REE-brum

The large wrinkly part. It helps with thinking, learning, senses, language, and voluntary movement.

Cerebellum

ser-uh-BELL-um

A smaller structure at the lower back of the brain. It is important for balance and coordinating movement.

Brainstem

BRAYN-stem

Connects the brain with the spinal cord and helps control vital functions such as breathing and heart rate.

⭐ Brain Scientist Check: Your brain is not just for thinking—it helps control your whole body.
🔬 Lesson 2: What is an MRI?

MRI stands for Magnetic Resonance Imaging. It uses a strong magnetic field and radio waves to make detailed pictures of structures inside the body.

Real MRI scanner — Cleveland Clinic
👀 What happens during a brain MRI?

A person lies on a table that slides into the large opening of the MRI scanner. The scanner collects signals that a computer uses to create detailed images.

It does not take just one ordinary photograph. It gives scientists information that can be viewed as many slices through the 3D brain.

The brain is a 3D object, but an MRI viewer can show it as many thin slices. Imagine a loaf of bread: the whole loaf is 3D, while each piece of bread is one slice.

🍉 Lesson 3: What is SAGITTAL?

Sagittal — SADGE-ih-tuhl

A sagittal plane runs from the front of the head to the back and separates the brain into left and right portions.

🍉 Imagine a watermelon facing forward. Make a vertical cut from its front to its back. Now you can look at it from the side. That is like a sagittal view.

Memory trick: Sagittal = Side. Both start with an S.

👇 In the MRI viewer below, the Sagittal window is the side view. Move the Left ↔ Right slider and watch the sagittal slice travel across the brain.
👑 Lesson 4: What is CORONAL?

Coronal — kuh-ROH-nuhl

A coronal plane is a vertical plane that separates the brain into front (anterior) and back (posterior) portions. You view the slice as though you are facing the brain.

👑 Corona means crown. Imagine a crown running from one ear over the top of your head to the other ear. A cut along that direction gives a coronal slice.

New scientist words: Anterior = toward the front; Posterior = toward the back.

👇 Find the Coronal window below. Move the Back ↔ Front slider and watch the slice travel through the brain.
🥯 Lesson 5: What is AXIAL?

Axial — AK-see-uhl

An axial plane is a horizontal plane that separates the brain into upper (superior) and lower (inferior) portions. It is also called a transverse plane.

🥯 Imagine making a horizontal cut through a bagel. Looking down at that cut surface is similar to looking at an axial brain slice.

New scientist words: Superior = toward the top; Inferior = toward the bottom.

👇 Find the Axial window below. Move the Bottom ↔ Top slider and watch the brain's shape change.
